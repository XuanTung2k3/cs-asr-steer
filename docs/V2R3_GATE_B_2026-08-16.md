# Gate B evidence — free decoding under oracle and non-oracle localization

**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Jobs:** Slurm 41142 (Task 0 repair, `COMPLETED`, 5 m 49 s) and 41162 (Gate B,
`COMPLETED`, exit 0, **29 m 44 s**); smokes 41143/41146–41149/41155
**Destinations:** `artifacts_dialogue_v2r3/invariance/generation_002_zh_repair`
and `artifacts_dialogue_v2r3/gate_b/generation_001`

## Purpose and boundary

Every effect measured before today lives in teacher-forced logprob space and is
localized by an oracle that hands the intervention the exact decoder step.
Neither condition exists at inference. This stage asks whether the frozen action
produces a net practical improvement on transcripts when the step must be found
from the model's own first-pass output.

It measures and reports. **No tier, layer, or strength was selected; no gate
criterion was evaluated; nothing was tuned to improve a number.** `D-dev-confirm`
was not read. `D-test` appears only as reference-only counts and a C00 baseline,
which are not intervention outcomes; no intervened condition was run or scored
there.

Frozen action, recorded and not re-derived: **Site D, decoder layer 16, ρ = 1.**

**The headline: none of the oracle effect survives inference-available
localization.** The gap is the whole oracle effect, and the reason is structural
rather than statistical.

## 1. Task 0 — the degenerate Day 6 cell, repaired

`jitter_zh −200 ms` reported exactly +0.0000 [0, 0] because the sign mapping
produced `control = onset − 1 − (−1) = onset`. The contrast
`block[onset] − block[control]` was then identically zero, so the "direction" was
the zero vector and correct/null margins were bit-identical.

The repair clamps the control step strictly before the onset under every offset
sign, in `control_step_for()`:

```python
return min(int(onset) - 1, int(onset) - 1 - int(control_offset))
```

| Cell | Offset | Before | After |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `jitter_zh −200 ms` | −1 | +0.0000 [+0.0000, +0.0000] | **+0.0915 [+0.0156, +0.1674]\*** G=19 |

`*` excludes zero. The repaired value sits inside the band of the other ZH cells
(+0.0827 … +0.1144). That is expected and is **not** independent evidence: the
clamp maps offset −1 onto the offset-0 construction, so this cell is a
null-seed replicate of the baseline construction rather than a jitter probe. A
Mandarin control cannot move *later* than the step immediately preceding the
onset, so positive-direction ZH jitter is structurally clamped.

**Nothing else moved**, established two ways:

1. *Deterministically* — replaying every ZH cell's recorded offset through the
   old and new rules changes only the −200 ms cell (offsets 0 and +1 are
   untouched by a clamp that binds only below zero).
2. *Empirically* — the repair job also recomputed the `inverse_variance` and
   `pooling` cells, and all **11,736 rows are bit-identical** to
   `generation_001` (max |diff| = 0.00e+00).

`generation_001` was not modified (mtime unchanged at 09:53:17); the repair was
published to a new path.

A regression test (`tests/test_v2r3_zh_control.py`, 11 tests) asserts
`control_step < onset` for every offset in the grid and carries a failing fixture
for the collapsed case. Under the pre-fix expression, offsets −1, −2, −3 all
violate the assertion.

**This is the headline number.**

| Tier | Oracle | Non-oracle | Gap (paired, clustered) | Fraction of oracle effect surviving |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 400 ms | 0.0143 (7/489) | 0.0000 (0/489) | **+0.0143 [+0.0033, +0.0254]\*** G=20 | **0.000** |
| 300 ms | 0.0139 (7/502) | 0.0000 (0/502) | +0.0139 [+0.0031, +0.0248]\* G=20 | 0.000 |
| 200 ms | 0.0136 (7/515) | 0.0000 (0/515) | +0.0136 [+0.0028, +0.0244]\* G=20 | 0.000 |

On PIER the gap is **−0.0060** at 400 ms (oracle 0.5189, non-oracle 0.5249,
baseline 0.5249): the non-oracle arm does not move PIER at all at the
conservative tier, and moves it by 0.0008 at 200 ms.

**None of the oracle effect survives inference-available localization.** The gap
is the entire oracle effect, and its interval excludes zero at every tier.

This rests on **7 corrections** in the oracle arm. The gap's CI excludes zero
because the non-oracle arm is exactly zero rather than because the oracle count
is large, and 7 events is below the ~20-event line at which I would treat a rate
as anything but descriptive. What is solid is the *direction and completeness* of
the loss; what is not established is the oracle rate's own precision.

## 4. Q2 — transport abstention, decomposed

Two failure modes stack. Day 5 established the first; the second is new.

At the 400 ms tier, over 489 targets:

| Category | Targets | No step at all | Step exists, **no English in hypothesis** | English present, below floor | Region found, not enriched | Localized | Transport hit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| deletion | 290 | **28.6%** | 37.7% | 33.8% | 14.0% | 10.3% | 1.0% |
| wrong-language substitution | 90 | 0% | **81.1%** | 13.3% | 0% | 5.6% | 3.3% |
| same-language substitution | 77 | 0% | 0% | 40.3% | 36.4% | 23.4% | 1.3% |
| phonetic/transliteration | 17 | 5.9% | 62.5% | 25.0% | 12.5% | 0% | 0% |
| boundary error | 12 | 0% | 0% | 33.3% | 33.3% | 33.3% | 0% |
| other | 3 | 0% | 0% | 33.3% | 66.7% | 0% | 0% |

**Overall: no step at all 17.2%; abstained 88.3%; localized 11.7%; transport hit
1.4%.**

The deletion column reproduces Day 5's 28.6% exactly — the decode terminates
before reaching the oracle step — and substitutions remain at 0%, as Day 5 found.

**The new mode dominates, and it is not a tuning problem.** For 37.7% of
deletions and **81.1% of wrong-language substitutions**, a decoder step exists but
the first-pass hypothesis **contains no English at all**. Transport asks which
step attends to the candidate English region; when the baseline error *is* the
disappearance of the English, there is no English in the hypothesis to attend to.
Two observed cases make the mechanism concrete:

- `social skills` → `社會技巧` and `improving` → `進步` — translated away;
- `Exercise your strength … generally` → dropped entirely.

You cannot localize what the model did not produce. This is the ceiling W3
inherits, and it sits *above* Day 5's missing-step ceiling rather than beside it.

Abstention as a function of threshold, so the rule can be re-read without another
run:

| Fixed cut on max_q r_q | 0.01 | 0.02 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| abstain rate | 0.707 | 0.716 | 0.810 | 0.905 | 0.940 | 0.983 |

The parameter-free enrichment rule actually used abstains at **0.836**.

## 5. Q3 — spillover: persistent, and sharply concentrated

At 400 ms, counted per utterance over 116 utterances:

| Quantity | Oracle | Non-oracle |
|---|---:|---:|
| units changed outside the target | **85** | **0** |
| improved | 72 | 0 |
| damaged | 13 | 0 |
| targeted corrections | 7 | 0 |
| off-target : targeted | **12.1×** | — |
| utterances with any spillover | 5 / 116 (4.3%) | 0 / 116 |

**It concentrates.** The per-utterance spillover counts are
**41, 32, 10, 1, 1** — the top **2 of 116 utterances account for 85.9%** of all
off-target change, and the top 3 for 97.6%.

**This answers the interpretation question in favour of an abstention rule.**
Spillover is not diffuse across the corpus; it is a property of a handful of
utterances. The selectivity claim is not refuted by a broad smear of collateral
change — it is threatened by a small recurring set that a router could learn to
decline. That is a method contribution rather than a defect, though it remains to
be shown that those utterances are identifiable *before* decoding.

The affected utterances are, by identifier:
`ZH-CN_U0027_S0_114`, `ZH-CN_U0040_S0_149`, `ZH-CN_U1003_S0_56`,
`ZH-CN_U1063_S0_17`, `ZH-CN_U1082_S0_60`.

**The two arms share none of them** — the non-oracle arm produces zero spillover,
because it abstains on 88.3% of targets and its interventions change no
transcript. The overlap question is therefore answered trivially and cannot
distinguish "the same utterances are fragile" from "the non-oracle arm does
nothing".

## 6. Task 3 — controls under free decoding

Conservative tier, non-oracle arm, the 57 non-abstaining targets (G = 11):

| Condition | Corrections | E_eff |
|---|---:|---:|
| correct direction | 0 / 57 | 17.4889 |
| label-permuted null (5 draws) | 0 / 285 | 1.5941 |
| matched-energy random (5 draws) | 0 / 285 | **17.4889** |

The matched-energy control's E_eff equals the correct direction's to four
decimals, as intended — the match is exact, not nominal, because it reuses the
same `r_q` and ρ with a direction of identical norm.

Paired contrasts against both controls are **+0.0000 [+0.0000, +0.0000], G = 11,
n = 57**.

**Stated plainly, as requested: the correct direction does not beat any null in
transcript space, because in the non-oracle arm it corrects nothing at all.** The
teacher-forced logprob effect (+0.106 at the frozen action, Day 4) does not
reproduce as transcript edits under inference-available localization. Logprob
margin and transcript edits are different outcome spaces, and the effect that
exists in the first does not appear in the second here.

The label-permuted control is not energy-matched (E_eff 1.59 vs 17.49) because
permuted directions have their own norms; that is a property of that control, and
the matched-energy random draw is the one that isolates direction from energy.

### Defects found, and what they cost

1. **Boundary jitter would have been vacuous** (found Day 6, before this run):
   the Site-D target derives from unit indices, so shifting span *times* never
   reaches it. Jitter now displaces the decoder step at the utterance's own token
   rate.
2. **A fixed abstention cut of `max_q r_q < 0.5` made the non-oracle arm vacuous
   by construction.** Whisper pads to 1500 frames and attends into the padding,
   so `r_q` never approaches 0.5 — the smoke reached 0.114. Replaced *before* the
   full run, and before any effect was known, with a parameter-free rule: the
   selected step must attend to the candidate region more than uniform chance.
3. **The enrichment baseline was apples-to-oranges.** `r_max` is a share of the
   whole attention row (padding included) while chance was computed inside the
   audio, so no step could clear it. Fixed to put both on the same denominator.
   Ties now abstain: a region covering the whole utterance gives enrichment
   exactly 1, and float noise was otherwise deciding the outcome.
4. **`tag_unit(str(unit))` tagged nothing as English.** `str(Unit(...))` is the
   repr, not the surface, so every embedded-English count silently became zero
   and every English unit was silently counted as monolingual material. Found by
   noticing D-test reported **0 embedded-English units across 6,257 utterances**.
   Fixed, regression-tested, and both affected quantities — monolingual retention
   and the D-test headroom — recomputed from the stored text. The corrected
   counts are independently confirmed by PIER, which uses its own
   reference-POI logic: `num_poi` = 35,921 and `num_poi_errors` = 10,458, exactly
   matching the recomputation. **Correction, corruption, spillover, transport, and
   control results were not affected**, as they never call `tag_unit`.

The `--only` cell filter added for Task 0 gates `run_cell` but not the
inverse-variance and pooling blocks, so the repair job recomputed those too. That
cost compute and produced the bit-identical cross-check reported in §1.

## 10. Open items

1. **Transport fails hardest where the intervention is aimed.** 81.1% of
   wrong-language substitutions and 37.7% of deletions have a step but no English
   in the first pass. Any localizer reading the first-pass hypothesis inherits
   this; a localizer reading the *audio* would not, but Site E is null.
2. **The non-oracle arm delivers more energy than the oracle arm where it acts**
   (17.49 vs 4.83) and still corrects nothing, so the null is a localization
   result, not a dose result.
3. **Corruptions exceed corrections in the oracle arm** (7 : 13 at 400 ms) once
   corruption has a proper denominator. Day 5's "0 corruptions" was an artefact
   of counting only inside a target set made entirely of baseline errors.
4. **Spillover concentrates in 2 of 116 utterances (85.9%).** Whether those
   utterances are identifiable before decoding decides whether an abstention rule
   is a method contribution or a post-hoc filter.
5. **`D-test` at G = 15 has MDE 0.0608**, roughly 4× the largest oracle effect
   measured. A confirmatory test on this split is underpowered for effects of the
   size in hand, independent of anything above.
